# Refund Policy

## Refund timelines
Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection.
Credit and debit card refunds can take an extra 2 to 3 days to appear on the statement, depending on the bank.

## Store credit
Customers can choose store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method. Store credit is available immediately after inspection and does not expire for 12 months.

## Partial refunds
A partial refund applies when an item is returned with missing accessories, visible wear or without original packaging. The deduction is between 10 and 40 percent of the item price, depending on the condition.

## Shipping fees
Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by our error, such as a damaged, defective or wrong item.

## Refunds for cancelled orders
Orders cancelled before shipping are refunded in full within 3 business days.
