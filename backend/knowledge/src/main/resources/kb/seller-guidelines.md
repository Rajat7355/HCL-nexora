# Seller Guidelines

## Listing quality
Listings must have accurate titles, at least three clear images, correct specifications and the real price. Misleading claims lead to delisting.

## Dispatch time
Sellers must dispatch orders within 48 hours of confirmation. Repeated late dispatch lowers the seller rating.

## Packaging
Items must be packed to survive transit. Electronics need protective inner packaging. Original manufacturer packaging must not be altered.

## Returns handled by sellers
Sellers must accept returns that follow the ShopSmart Return Policy and cannot add extra fees.
