# Shipping Policy

## Delivery options
Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available in major cities.
Customers can set a delivery preference in their profile.

## Shipping fees
Basic members pay a shipping fee of ₹580.23 on orders below ₹9,686.72; orders of ₹9,686.72 or more ship free.
Prime members get free standard shipping on every order. Premium members get free standard and express shipping on every order.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed before the order is packed. After the order is packed or shipped the address cannot be changed.

## Tracking
Every shipment has a tracking number and a live timeline on the Orders page. Tracking is updated at each handover between hubs.

## International orders
International orders are delivered by DHL Express in 5 to 10 business days. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer.

## Cancellation before shipping
Orders can be cancelled until they ship. After shipping, cancellation is not possible and the return policy applies.
