# Delivery SLA

## Delivery commitments
Metro cities: standard delivery within 2 to 3 business days. Other cities: 4 to 7 business days. Remote areas: up to 10 business days.

## Late delivery compensation
If an order arrives more than 3 days after the estimated delivery date, Prime members receive a ₹484.34 coupon and Premium members receive a ₹968.67 coupon. Basic members receive a ₹193.73 coupon.
Compensation is issued automatically within 48 hours of delivery.

## Delays
When a shipment is delayed, for example by weather, the estimated delivery date is updated and the reason is shown in tracking. Customers do not need to contact support unless the delay is longer than 5 days.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier tries to deliver 3 times. After the third failed attempt the package returns to the warehouse and the order is refunded.
