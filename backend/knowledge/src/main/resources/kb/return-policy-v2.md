# Return Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging.
Electronics such as laptops, smartphones and headphones can be returned within 15 days of delivery.
Opened electronics are accepted only if they are not damaged and all accessories are included.

## Items that cannot be returned
Items that cannot be returned: gift cards, downloadable software, perishable goods, personalised or custom-made products, and items marked as final sale.
Earbuds and in-ear headphones cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, unless they are defective.

## How to start a return
Open the Orders page, choose the order and select Request return. A prepaid return label is emailed within 24 hours. Prime and Premium members get free return pickup. Basic members pay a flat return shipping fee of ₹483.37, which is waived if the item is defective or wrong.

## Order cancellation
An order can be cancelled from the Orders page until it has shipped. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but the customer can refuse the delivery or return the item after it arrives. Cancelled orders are refunded in full.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, defective or different from the listing, report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. We arrange a free replacement or a full refund, and return shipping is free.

## Inspection
Returned items are inspected at our warehouse within 3 business days of arrival. The refund starts after the inspection passes.
