# Laptop Care and Setup Guide

## First setup
Charge the laptop to full before first use. Complete the operating system setup and install pending updates. Register the product on the manufacturer site to activate the warranty.

## Battery care
Keep battery charge between 20 and 80 percent for daily use. Avoid leaving the laptop in a hot car. Use only the charger supplied or a certified equivalent.

## Troubleshooting
If the laptop does not turn on, hold the power button for 15 seconds, plug in the charger for 30 minutes and try again. If the screen stays black, connect an external display to check whether the problem is the screen or the system.
If the fans are loud, clean the air vents and close heavy background applications.
