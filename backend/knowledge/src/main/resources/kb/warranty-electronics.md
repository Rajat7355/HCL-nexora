# Electronics Warranty Terms

## Standard warranty
Laptops, smartphones, headphones and smart home devices carry a 12 month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures that happen during normal use.

## Extended warranty for Premium members
Premium members get 24 months of warranty on laptops and smartphones bought on ShopSmart. Accidental damage cover is not included.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repairs, cosmetic wear, battery capacity loss below normal ageing, or damage caused by using the wrong charger.

## How to claim
Contact support with the order number and a short description of the fault. We either arrange a free repair or send a replacement. Repairs take 7 to 10 business days.
Keep the invoice, because it is the proof of purchase for every warranty claim.
