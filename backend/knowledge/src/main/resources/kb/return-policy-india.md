# Return Policy India Addendum

## Return window in India
For orders delivered in India the return window for electronics is 10 days from delivery, instead of the 15 days that apply elsewhere.
Other categories keep the standard 30 day window.

## Cash on delivery refunds
Refunds for cash on delivery orders in India are paid by bank transfer. The customer must provide bank account details in the return request. Transfers take 5 to 7 business days.

## Pickup
Return pickup in India is arranged by our courier partners within 2 to 3 working days of the request.
